# Gameplay guide

## Controls (touch, landscape)

On foot:
- Left joystick — move (magnitude scales walk/run)
- Right side of screen — drag to rotate camera
- JUMP, RUN (hold), FIRE (when armed), DUCK, ENTER buttons
- ENTER — enter nearest vehicle, or open a shop when standing in one

Driving:
- Joystick — steer (up/down also give throttle/brake)
- GAS / BRAKE buttons, DRIFT (handbrake), HORN, EXIT buttons
- Camera auto-aligns behind the vehicle; drag to look around

Desktop (testing): WASD move, mouse drag (right-drag on the look area) look,
Space jump, Shift sprint, F enter/exit, E interact, left-click fire, Esc pause.

## Core loop

1. Main menu → **DOWNLOAD ASSETS** (first run only, ~1 GB) → NEW GAME.
2. Follow mission markers (yellow cylinders, also shown on the minimap).
3. Earn money from missions, hidden packages and races; spend it on weapons,
   clothes, armor, repairs and garage storage.
4. Crime raises your **wanted level** (1–5 stars). Police cars pursue; officers
   dismount and shoot when you stop. Break line of sight and stay away to lose
   them — the stars decay one level at a time.
5. Death sends you to the hospital (-$100) and fails the active mission.

## Systems

- **Day/night** (8 min cycle) and **weather** change on their own; storms bring
  lightning. Headlights and street lights come on at night.
- **Swimming**: the river east of downtown and the ocean are swimmable — drive or
  fall in and Marcus floats and swims.
- **Vehicles**: take damage from collisions (smoke < 45%, fire + explosion at 0),
  can be repaired at gas stations ($50), and stored in the garage.
- **Collectibles**: 30 packages hidden around the city ($100 each).
- **Races**: story mission 5 and repeatable street races.
